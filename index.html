HOW TO PLAY

The rules of Kalah

Two players. Six pockets each. Collect more stones in your store than your opponent.

  1. Set up. Each of the twelve small pockets starts with four stones. The two large stores start empty. Player 1 moves first. Each player owns one row and its store to the right when viewed from that player’s side.
  2. Sow the stones. On your turn, choose any nonempty pocket on your side. Pick up all its stones, then place one in each next pocket, counterclockwise. Include your own store and skip your opponent’s store. Keep going around the board if needed, including your starting pocket on a later lap. Stones already in a store are never played again.
  3. Take an extra move. If your last stone lands in your own store, move again. Extra turns can continue as long as you keep finishing in your store, unless the game has ended.
  4. Capture. If your last stone lands in an empty pocket on your own side, and the pocket directly opposite contains stones, put that last stone and all stones opposite into your store. If the opposite pocket is empty, there is no capture. Landing on the opponent’s side never captures.
  5. Finish the game. As soon as either player’s six pockets are all empty, the game ends. The other player puts all stones remaining on their own side into their own store. This happens even if the last move would otherwise give an extra turn.
  6. Count the stores. More than 24 stones wins; 24–24 is a draw. Having 25 stones already guarantees a win, but you may keep exploring to see the exact final total.
